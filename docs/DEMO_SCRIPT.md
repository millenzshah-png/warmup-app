# Demo script

A walkthrough for showing the app to judges. It takes about four minutes.
Run `npm run dev`, open the app on a laptop, and keep the demo controls on the
right in view. Everything below is live: nothing is a recording.

Before you start, tap **Reset the demo** at the bottom of the demo controls so
you begin at the welcome screen.

## 1. Setup (30 seconds)

1. **Get started**, then **Continue**.
2. The pairing screen finds "Warmup Hoodie · M" with both cell levels.
   Tap **Connect**.
3. Name it, pick a size, tap **Done**.

Say: "The hoodie tells the app its size and how much charge each cell has
before you have even connected."

## 2. Heat that thinks for you (45 seconds)

1. Home shows the skin temperature from the collar sensor, live.
2. Point at the status line: **Auto · Medium**, holding 93°F.
3. Tap **High**. The runtime drops, the glow brightens.
4. Watch the two chest zones. Once the target is held they pulse on and off.

Say: "It is not a dial. It reads your skin and only spends battery when you
need it. That pulsing is why the cells last longer than the level suggests."

## 3. Safety (45 seconds)

1. In the demo controls tap **Put on a backpack**.
2. Wait about 40 seconds, or set the speed to **10×**.
3. The **Heat paused** sheet appears: the back panel reached 113°F and was cut
   off. The reading counts down as it cools.
4. Tap **Backpack on** to take it off, then **Resume when safe**.

Say: "Each panel has its own cutoff, on the hoodie, not in the app. If it
trips three times in ten minutes it locks that zone for the day."

## 4. Cells (30 seconds)

1. Tap the battery pill on Home to open **Battery**.
2. In the demo controls set the left cell to **Out**.
3. A notice says it is running on the other cell. The left ring greys out.
4. Set it to **Dock** and switch to **60×**. It charges while you watch.
5. Set it back to **In** and return to **1×**.

Say: "Two 50 gram cells in the side seams. Pull one out and it keeps going."

## 5. Modes and schedule (45 seconds)

1. **Modes** tab. Tap **Sick**: slow ramp, 95°F, off after two hours.
2. Tap **New mode**, drag the target, switch zones, **Save mode**.
3. Settings, **Schedules**. Switch **Preheat before I leave** on.
4. In the demo controls tap **Jump to 7:09 AM**. A minute later the hoodie
   starts the preheat by itself and the app says so.

Say: "The schedule lives on the hoodie, so it preheats even with the phone
asleep."

## 6. Update (30 seconds)

1. Settings, **Firmware update**, **Install now**.
2. The ring runs through Downloading, Installing, Verifying and Restarting.
3. Open **Battery** afterwards. On 1.3.0 the two cells drain at the same rate.

Say: "That was a real transfer: 412 KB in chunks, checked by the hoodie
before it installs. A damaged image is refused."

## 7. Storefront (15 seconds)

Open **Shop** from Settings. Pick a colour and a graphic and the price updates.
The phone on that page is the live app.

## If something looks stuck

- Nothing on Home is moving: check the speed is not at 600×, then set it to 1×.
- "Reconnecting": a cell is out. Set one back to **In**.
- A zone says "Off for today": switch it back on from the **Zones** tab.
- To start over: **Reset the demo**.
